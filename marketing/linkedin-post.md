# LinkedIn Post — LifeOS

## Option 1 — Story-driven (recommended)

🚀 I built an AI-powered operating system for my life — and it's live.

Meet **LifeOS**: tasks, calendar, notes, ideas, reminders, goals, projects, focus timer, and a full voice assistant — in ONE app.

Here's what makes it different 👇

🤖 **A real AI assistant with full app control**
Not a chatbot bolted on the side. Talk to it and it DOES things: "add a task for tomorrow 6pm high priority," "show my ideas," "capture an idea with photo," "what's due today?" — it creates, completes, deletes, reschedules, and navigates every single section of the app.

🎙️ **Hands-free, always listening**
Works even when the app is closed. Native Android speech engine, wake-word detection, spoken replies.

💡 **Instant idea capture**
An idea strikes? Capture it with a photo AND a voice note — frame the shot, talk about it, save. Never lose a 2am thought again.

📅 **Smart calendar that plans itself**
Tasks with times, schedule blocks, reminders, and milestones — all layered on one calendar. Recurring tasks just work.

📊 **Insights that actually help**
Completion rates, 14-day trends, most productive days, breakdowns by priority and category, focus-time stats.

⏱️ **Focus mode**
Pomodoro timer tied to a real task. One task, a timer, nothing else.

🌙 **10 languages, premium themes, dark mode**
English, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic (full RTL), Russian.

🔄 **Offline-first sync**
Every change syncs across devices — even offline. Android APK + web app.

📦 **Export everything** — your data belongs to you.

Built solo with React, TypeScript, Supabase, and a custom Android WebView shell with native bridges for speech, alarms, and camera.

Try it live: https://life-os-tdl.vercel.app
📱 Android APK available on the download page.

## Tips for posting

1. **Attach the video** — LinkedIn auto-plays videos in feed; the app tour grabs attention in the first 2 seconds (Kage dark theme looks premium).
2. **Post timing:** Tue–Thu, 9–11 AM in your timezone gets best reach for tech content.
3. **First 2 lines matter** — LinkedIn truncates after ~2 lines with "see more", so the hook line is the most important part.
4. **Video length:** if it's over 90 seconds, consider trimming to the dashboard → voice moment → calendar flow. Retention drops hard after 90s.
5. **Reply to every comment in the first hour** — the algorithm rewards early engagement heavily.
